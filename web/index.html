Senior citizen and PWD discount till and sales register for restaurants

The sale is VAT-exempt first and the 20% discount comes off the amount net of VAT — not off the posted price. This till does it in that order, splits a shared bill per head, and keeps the sales record with the period total of discounts granted.

Restaurants, caterers, homestays, small hotels, tour operators RA 9994 · RA 10754 · BIR RR 7-2010 / RR 5-2017

The bill

Whether a good or service is covered is set by RA 9994, RA 10754 and their implementing rules — mark each line yourself. “Covered, no VAT” is for a line that carries no VAT in its own right.
